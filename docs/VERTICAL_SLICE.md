# Vertical Slice — Day One at North Valley State

One sentence: *A mobile college-life RPG where the player completes their first
day at a fictional university and makes choices that affect academics, energy,
relationships, and athletic opportunity.*

## Flow

```
Title / Continue
    ↓
Character Creation (name + identity)
    ↓
Morning   — Dorm arrival, meet roommate Jordan Hayes
    ↓
Afternoon — Class, open gym, or explore campus
    ↓
Evening   — Meet Coach Delgado at the rec center
    ↓
Night     — Study, hang out, or sleep
    ↓
Day One Recap (personalized, shareable)
```

## Rules for the slice
- Every period presents exactly one story beat with 2–4 choices.
- Every choice changes at least two stats and is recorded for the recap.
- At least one choice must be gated by a requirement (energy) so the player
  feels the constraint system working.
- The recap must reference specific choices, not just final numbers.
- Save happens automatically after every choice; relaunching mid-day resumes.

## Definition of "this slice is done"
See DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md. The headline test: a playtester finishes Day 1 and
says "I want to try again and make different choices."

## Explicitly out of scope for the slice
Days 2–7, 3D scenes, movement, the tryout minigame, audio, any second sport.
